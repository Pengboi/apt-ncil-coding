Section A: Reading (40 marks)

Read the following extract carefully before answering the questions.

Extract from a story by a modern author:

The old house stood at the edge of the cliff, its windows like empty eyes staring out to sea. For fifty years, no one had lived there. The locals called it Blackwood Manor and crossed the road to avoid walking past its rusted gates. But Emily wasn't superstitious. She was an estate agent with a quota to meet, and this listing represented her biggest commission yet.

As she stepped onto the gravel driveway, the crunch beneath her feet seemed unnaturally loud. The wind whipped her coat around her legs, and she noticed how the house seemed to lean toward her, as if listening. The front door hung slightly ajar—that hadn't been in the property description. She should have called the caretaker. She should have waited for daylight.

"Hello?" Her voice sounded small against the crash of waves below. "Anyone here?"

The hallway smelled of salt and something else—something metallic and ancient. Her torch beam caught dust motes dancing in the air, and for a moment, she thought she saw footprints in the grime, fresh footprints leading up the stairs.
