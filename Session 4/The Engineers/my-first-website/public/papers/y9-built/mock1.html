Year 9 Built Environment

Architectural Design Assessment

Paper 1: Design & Planning Time: 1 hour Marks: 50 Tier: Foundation

Instructions

  • Answer all questions in the spaces provided.
  • Drawings should be done in pencil with clear labels.
  • Show all measurements and scale clearly.
Question 1 4 marks

(a) What does scale 1:50 mean in architectural drawing?

(b) If a room is 5m wide in real life, how wide would it be on a 1:50 scale drawing?

Question 2 6 marks

(a) Name and describe two different types of foundations used in construction. [4]

(b) Why are foundations important in building construction? [2]

Question 3 8 marks

Explain three features of sustainable building design and how they help the environment.

Question 4 12 marks

Design a small eco-friendly house (single storey, 2 bedrooms). Your design should include:

  • Floor plan drawn to scale (use 1:50)
  • Front elevation sketch
  • List of sustainable features included
  • Materials selection with reasons
Question 5 6 marks

A new housing development is being planned near your school.

(a) List three stakeholders who should be consulted. [3]

(b) What is one potential concern local residents might have? [1]

(c) How could the developers address this concern? [2]

END OF PAPER
