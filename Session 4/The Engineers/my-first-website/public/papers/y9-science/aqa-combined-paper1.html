Formulae

Speed = distance ÷ time
Density = mass ÷ volume
Force = mass × acceleration
Work done = force × distance
Power = work done ÷ time
Weight = mass × gravitational field strength (g = 10 N/kg)

PHYSICS - Forces and Energy
Question 7 6 marks

A car travels 150 metres in 10 seconds.

(a) Calculate the speed of the car. [2]

Use the equation: speed = distance ÷ time

(b) The car then accelerates from 15 m/s to 25 m/s in 5 seconds.

(i) Calculate the acceleration of the car. [2]

Use the equation: acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time

(ii) Describe what happens to the velocity of the car during this acceleration. [2]

Question 8 4 marks

Block Mass (g) Volume (cm³)

(a) Calculate the density of block A. [2]

(b) Explain why all three blocks have the same density. [2]

Question 9 6 marks

A student lifts a box weighing 50 N up a height of 2 metres.

(a) Calculate the work done by the student. [2]

Use the equation: work done = force × distance

(b) If the student takes 4 seconds to lift the box, calculate the power developed. [2]

Use the equation: power = work done ÷ time

(c) The student then lifts a heavier box of 80 N to the same height in the same time. Explain what happens to the power needed. [2]
