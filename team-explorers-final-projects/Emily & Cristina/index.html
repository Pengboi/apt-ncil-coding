Cinnamnoroll

Cinnamoroll (シナモロール, Shinamorōru) is a small, chubby puppy created by Sanrio, making his debut in 2001. He is characterized by his white fur, blue eyes, pink cheeks, and long ears that allow him to fly. His tail resembles a cinnamon roll, which is the inspiration behind his name. Cinnamoroll was born on March 6 on a cloud high in the sky and fell to Earth near Café Cinnamon, where he was discovered by a kind café owner named Ms. Anna.
