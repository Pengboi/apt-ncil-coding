LLMs

LLMs are a type of AI like a chatbot which uses things like:
• AI models which are the main next-word predictors and manages most of the LLM's calculations
• RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) sharpens the accuracy of an AI model.
• Massive datasets to train all parameters and strengthen the LLM's knowledge of word connections.
• The transformer of an LLM is the neural architecture which includes of the input layer, the hidden layer and the output layer.

The main and most powerful LLM is Gemini.
